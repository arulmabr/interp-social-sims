3. WHAT WAS FOUND
================================================================================

3.1 THE READOUT DOES NOT TRACK FREE GENERATION. Sampled generation is primary.
    The instrument check (FRIDAY amendment item 2) compared the logit readout
    against G2's free generation at the same grid points. The bar was crossings
    within one grid step and a slope in 0.8-1.2.

      coding     readout crossing   free crossing   gap      slope    r
      legacy           234.1             62.6      34 steps  0.751  0.26
      strict           234.1            110.0      25 steps  0.268  0.16
      decision         234.1             63.2      34 steps  0.959  0.31

    Every row fails. Under the standing rule, **sampled generation is primary
    and the readout is a secondary measurement.** The decision-coding slope sits
    inside the band, but with r = 0.31 that is a slope through noise.

3.2 THE AS-WRITTEN VARIANT PRODUCES NO USABLE DOSE-RESPONSE CURVE.
    Llama, layer 48, probe direction, lottery, under the coding that requires an
    explicitly stated decision: **97-99% no answer at every dose**, from zero to
    1.0 of the median residual norm. The few crossings that appear come from the
    `legacy` and `strict` codings, which resolve by label position and so read
    the prompt's own option ordering. The readout never crosses 0.5 on the grid.
    Run exactly as committed, the probe pipeline yields nothing to fit a curve to.
    This corroborates G2 from the steered side.

3.3 CODING AGREEMENT IS A PROPERTY OF THE TOKEN CAP, NOT OF THE CODING.
      G2 chat64, 64 new tokens      0.591
      G4 as-written, 12 new tokens  0.982 over 27,120 trials per replica
    Twelve tokens leave no room to reason, so there is one label in the text and
    both rules read it. Neither figure is quoted without the trials on which both
    codings produced a choice, that share, and both no-answer rates.

3.4 QWEN's CHOICE PROBE SHARES A DIRECTION WITH A PAYOFF CODE.
    G4q, layer 17, rebuilt probe:

                      lottery   ultimatum
      probe accuracy    0.898     0.931
      reward probe      1.000     1.000
      cos(choice, reward)  +0.590    +0.540
      cos(choice, diffmean) +0.766   +0.668
      cos(choice, pca1)     +0.447   +0.447
      cos(choice, ridge log n) +0.510 +0.765

    A probe for "is the payoff above the grid median" is **perfectly accurate**,
    above the choice probe's own accuracy. The cosine null for this hidden size
    is about 0.017, so +0.59 is roughly 35x the scale of two unrelated
    directions. This is the extension plan's hypothesis 1 measured directly for
    the first time. It does not settle it -- 0.59 is shared direction, not
    identity -- but it is a number rather than a structural argument.

3.5 THE TOKEN CAP IS THE WHOLE STORY FOR THE CHAT TEMPLATE.
      cap  64   decision rate 0.707   truncated 0.343
      cap 128   decision rate 0.821   truncated 0.264
      cap 256   decision rate 1.000   truncated 0.000
    G4's sampled arm runs at 256 rather than inheriting 64.

3.6 EARLIER FINDINGS, UNCHANGED. The committed probe path answers nothing on
    88.3% of Llama lottery trials while the published data has 0 unparseable
    rows in 21,040; the four probe files postdate their runs by seven months;
    the preference probe sees 45 prompts differing in one integer and the
    capability probe two; the legacy hook steers zero generated tokens; the 0.77
    judge agreement is a pooling artefact (0.135 and 0.020 within the prompting
    condition); Llama under the chat template accepts 760 of 760 ultimatum
    offers; V1 finds no scoring pass that reproduces Figure 5 (21 matches across
    1,175 cells against 21.0 expected by chance, and no coherent pair); V2 finds
    the five-judge passes carry no API response ids.

4. INCIDENTS: WHAT WENT WRONG AND WHAT CHANGED
================================================================================

Since about 22:00 UTC most of the effort went into the scaffolding, not the
science. Eleven deviations are logged (D-2026-09-21-00 .. -11) and decisions run
to D68.

4.1 I CANCELLED SEVEN OF TRACK B'S JOBS, TWO MID-RUN.  (D-2026-09-21-11)
    b3_lora8b3 lost 15 min 20 s and b3_lora8b4 lost 18 min 39 s of LoRA anchor
    training -- the task the authors had said was not this session's to build.
    Cause: rebuilding my queue with `squeue | grep cvb_ | xargs scancel`. The
    `cvb_` prefix is shared by both sessions so the GPU-hour caps read off one
    sacct total: it is a **budget** key and I used it as an **ownership** key. On
    a preemptable partition a running job drops to pending constantly, so a Track
    B job with 18 minutes of work looked exactly like a stale submission of mine.
    This also broke rule 8. `cv_bench/mine.py` is now the only sanctioned answer
    to "which jobs may I cancel"; it refuses anything matching ^cvb_b\d and was
    verified in both directions. No prefix sweep is used again. Track B's work
    was not touched afterwards to remediate -- that would repeat the error.

4.2 FIVE MECHANISMS THAT TURNED A FAILURE INTO A SILENCE.
    * a dependency that fired on cancellation (afterany where afterok was meant),
      releasing G3 against a framing selection built from zero rows;
    * a selection written from zero free-generation levels, which parsed cleanly;
    * `srun ...` followed by `echo exit=$?`, so the echo's status became the
      script's and a crashed job reported COMPLETED to sacct;
    * only the last srun propagating, so a two-phase job whose first phase
      crashed reported COMPLETED in 24 seconds;
    * a failure watcher whose sacct query used UTC on a cluster that runs EDT,
      so it errored and returned nothing -- which looks exactly like "all fine".
    All fixed and, where testable, tested. The guards themselves all worked: the
    placement assertion, the empty-selection refusal and the completion markers
    each fired. What failed was the reporting path between the guard and me.

4.3 ONE BAD NODE ACCOUNTED FOR FIVE FAILURES.  (D68)
    node2119: cvb_g3, cvb_g4b, cvb_g5 twice, cvb_neutral2, with two different
    CUDA faults. Every other failing node failed once, from a capacity fault now
    fixed. `--exclude=node2119` everywhere. cvb_g5 failed four times before this
    and succeeded on the fifth attempt, on a different node.

4.4 A GPU REQUEST THAT DID NOT FIT, AND A LOADER THAT NOW REFUSES TO PRETEND.
    `--gres=gpu:2` can land on two L40S: 80.1 GiB usable against 141.1 GB
    needed, so 36 layers went to disk. Every 70B job now asks for a100:2
    (142.6 GiB). Earlier the same class of fault put the whole model on the CPU
    while the job looked healthy; `load()` now waits for GPU memory, caps CPU at
    0 GiB so accelerate must place on a GPU or raise, asserts the device map,
    and retries a busy-device probe rather than dying on it.

4.5 CORRECTIONS TO PUBLISHED NUMBERS.
    The decision detector missed this model's own convention -- reason, then the
    answer alone on the last line. Recoded from saved text, no rerun:
    decision rate 0.31/0.34/0.34 by cap became 0.41/0.64/1.00, and
    reports/G2_RESULTS.md's claim that the rate was "identical" at 12 and 64
    tokens became 32.0% and 41.3%. The neutral free generation ran over a range
    entirely below the switching point, making a constant result inevitable;
    that selection is withdrawn and the rerun spans the crossing.

6. GOVERNING RULES AS THEY NOW STAND
================================================================================

CLAUDE.md carries rules 1-12, the 22 Sept amendment to rules 6 and 7, and
**rule 16: autonomy with four hard stops** -- a pre-registered threshold or
metric, an anchor's training objective after its dry run passed, the GPU budget
(400 here to the cutoff, 1,000 for Track B through 5 Nov), and paid API spend
above the standing $50. Everything else: keep every threshold and lock intact,
choose the option cheapest to reverse, record it with the alternatives, proceed.

The test-split unlock is now a 24-hour veto window after the prereg-v1 tag.
Silence is consent under that rule, so the window's opening and expiry are
posted in logs/DECISIONS.md when it opens and nothing is touched before the full
24 hours elapse. The tag does not yet exist.

Unchanged: no number without a file behind it; the legacy directories are
read-only; the outcome never decides a rerun; every arm that runs is reported;
secrets are never printed or committed; the authors decide the paper text.
